# Privacy Policy — PocketWise

**Last updated: September 16, 2026**

## Overview

PocketWise is a personal expense tracker. Your data belongs to you. This policy explains what we collect, what we don't, and how your data is handled.

## Data We Collect

PocketWise stores the following data **locally on your device**:

- Expense records (amount, category, description, date)
- Categories you create
- Recurring expense rules
- App preferences (currency, budget, app lock setting)

## Google Drive Backup

If you choose to enable backup, PocketWise stores a copy of your expense data in a **private, sandboxed folder** in your personal Google Drive (`appDataFolder`). This folder is:

- Accessible only by PocketWise — no other app or person can read it
- Not visible in Google Drive's regular file browser
- Under your full control — you can delete it at any time from Google Account settings

We do not have access to your Google Drive or your backup data.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect any analytics or usage data
- We do not transmit your expense data to any server we operate
- We do not share your data with any third party
- We do not use advertising or tracking

## Voice Logging

If you use the voice expense feature, a short audio recording is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to parse the expense details. This is governed by Google's privacy policy. No recording is stored by PocketWise after processing.

## Data Deletion

All your data is stored on your device. To delete it, simply uninstall the app. To delete your Google Drive backup, go to your Google Account → Data & privacy → Third-party apps → PocketWise → Remove access.

## Contact

If you have questions, open an issue at the app's GitHub repository.
